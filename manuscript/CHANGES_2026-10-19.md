## Numbers and their sources

RMSE columns are in ft2/ac (BA) and ft3/ac (VOL_CFGRS, gross cubic foot volume); the manuscript convention "ft2/ac" was kept. Bootstrap intervals for percent RMSE reduction are computed as 100*(BA_RMSE_default - BA_RMSE_hi_calib)/BA_RMSE_default to 100*(BA_RMSE_default - BA_RMSE_lo_calib)/BA_RMSE_default. The CSV intervals are 95% percentile intervals from 1,000 resamples of conditions on the calibrated statistic only (per the caller; source script 19_fia_benchmark_engine.R bootstrap_stat_ci was not in the evidence folder), so these intervals treat the default RMSE as fixed and are not a paired bootstrap of the difference.
