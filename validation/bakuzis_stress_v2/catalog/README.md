# CONUS configuration catalog for fvs-modern (October 28, 2026)

**Status: shipped under the primary rule on October 29, 2026 (Aaron's decision, 14:36 EDT).** `recommended` and `status` in `config/catalog/` follow the primary rule; the alternative rule stays visible in `admissible_alt` and `rule_sensitive`. The stress test of the shipped verdicts is in `stress/` (see the section below).

This folder holds the evidence and the rule behind `config/catalog/`, which `fvs2py.catalog.select_config(variant)` reads. The catalog answers one question per FVS variant: which survival model (the variant's native mortality or GOMPIT crown-closure survival) and which parameter set (fvs-modern default, fvs-modern Bayesian calibrated MAP, or for comparison the CONUS trait-mediated projector arms b1 and b2) stays inside the observed envelope of the FIA fitting data over a 50 and a 100 year projection, and which of the admissible arms is the simplest.

## Common stand sample

For every variant, up to 300 FIA plots were drawn (seed 20261028) from the fitting data's own variant assignment, the plot-level roll-up of `conus_remeasurement_pairs_metric_cond_v2_cspiv6.rds` (`fia_variant_assignment.csv`, 17 variants with plots; PN 38, CA 142, TT 142, WC 226, all others 300). The Acadian variant uses the Northeast-assigned plots in Maine, New Hampshire and Vermont, an assumption, since the pairs file carries no ACD assignment. Variants with no plots in the pairs file (KT, OC, OP, NC for the engine runs) are reported as not evaluated rather than filled from another source. Each plot's first-measurement condition was written to FVS_StandInit and FVS_TreeInit through the repaired `fia_stand_generator.py` (PR 117: `basal_area_factor -1`, `inv_plot_size 1`, `brk_dbh 999`, so TPA_UNADJ passes through), with the public FIA plot coordinate, the condition's site species (SISP) where present, and the variant's USFS region. The same stands were projected 100 years at a 5 year step under four engine arms, default and calibrated MAP by native and GOMPIT survival, with no regeneration keywords and no posterior draws (EM and IE run with NOAUTOES, since their establishment models otherwise add stems in every projection; no other variant establishes regeneration). Trees are those of the sampled condition, expanded to the condition acre by CONDPROP_UNADJ (the definition of BALIVE), with species the compiled variant does not carry relabelled to its other-softwood (FIA 299) or other-hardwood (998) code as FVS itself does on input. A year-0 gate records whether each stand entered the engine within 20 percent of its FIA live basal area, and only stands that pass it enter the gates.

The CONUS projector arms (b1 and b2 by native and GOMPIT) come from the October 27 rescore (`cardinal_rescore/`), 1,500 stands per variant drawn from the projector's own stand set. They are listed in the same gate table for comparison, with the caveat that they were not run on the common sample and not through this engine, so differences between them and the engine arms confound engine with parameter set.

## Thresholds

Observed envelopes are plot-level percentiles of the fitting data by variant (`fia_variant_thresholds_plot.csv`): basal area p50, p90, p99 and p99.9 in m² ha⁻¹ and the SDI p99.9 in trees ha⁻¹ (the file's per-acre SDI converted). They are age-censored population extremes, so a variant whose inventory is young or managed (SN, CS, PN plantations) has a lower ceiling than its unmanaged stands could reach; the 50 year gate limits how far that bias reaches, and the 100 year shares are reported beside it.

## Pre-registered rule (fixed before any catalog run was scored; the plan red team's repairs are folded in)

An arm is admissible for a variant when all four hold: fewer than 5 percent of stands exceed the BA p99.9 by year 50 and the Wilson 95 percent upper bound of that share is under 10 percent; the same for the SDI p99.9; the Reineke flag rate among trajectories evaluated above the relative density gate (SDI/SDImax 0.55) is under 10 percent, not gated when fewer than 10 trajectories are evaluated (which is how TT default GOMPIT is admitted on 5 evaluated trajectories); and the median peak basal area over 100 years is at or above the FIA p50, which catches an arm that passes the ceilings by killing the stand. Among admissible arms the simplest wins (default before calibrated before b1 before b2; native before GOMPIT), except where per-variant near-term evidence separates the survival models, which at present is only the Northeast cohort of Paper 2 (basal area bias +10.78 percent native against +4.62 percent GOMPIT), so NE prefers GOMPIT among its admissible arms. A variant with no admissible arm is NOT RECOMMENDED for long-horizon use and the least-bad arm by 50 year BA exceedance is named for information.

## Repairs after the October 29 output red team

Four defects in the first catalog were confirmed and repaired before the catalog was regenerated, and are logged in `implementation-notes.md`: the under-stocking bound used the 100 year peak basal area, which includes year 0 and could not fail, and is now the median basal area at year 50 against the FIA p50; a Reineke gate that could not be tested (fewer than 10 evaluated trajectories) counted as a pass and now counts as NOT TESTED, which admits nothing; stands that failed the year-0 seed gate were scored and are now excluded, with the Reineke counts restricted to the same stands; and a calibrated arm whose trajectories equal the default arm's is marked `calibrated_distinct = FALSE` and is not evidence (this caught ACD and IE, whose keyword loader raised on the variants' per-species BAMAX array; the loader now skips that array and both were rerun). Two disclosures were added: the p99.9 threshold is the sample maximum wherever the variant has fewer than 1,000 fitting plots (`threshold_n` in the gate table), and the projector b1 GOMPIT arm has no NE or SN rows in the October 27 rescore.

## Stress-test alternative and rule sensitivity

The protocol's alternative Reineke gate, added after the interim scoring showed the western pattern and before the final scoring, uses the Wilson 95 percent upper bound of the flag rate under 25 percent with at least 30 evaluated trajectories; fewer evaluated trajectories is NOT TESTED under both rules. `catalog_gates_by_arm.csv` carries both verdicts (`admissible`, `admissible_alt`) and `catalog_recommendation_by_variant.csv` flags `rule_sensitive` where the admissible sets differ (eight variants). The primary rule is what `recommended` and `status` report; the alternative admits western arms by declining to test their self-thinning, which is why it is shown and not shipped as the default.

## Results in one paragraph (October 29, regenerated catalog)

Eighteen variants ran; 4,485 stands passed the year-0 seed gate and carry the gates (95 to 100 percent of sampled stands in 15 variants, 89 percent in LS, 78 in CS, 54 in SN, where the failing eastern stands enter above BALIVE at a median 1.46 times and are excluded, unexplained). Under the primary rule seven variants have admissible fvs-modern arms (ACD, BM, CS, NE, SN on all four; LS on the two calibrated arms; WS calibrated native) and eleven do not (CA, CI, CR, EC, EM, IE, PN, SO, TT, UT, WC), nearly all on the Reineke axis alone; the magnitude gates pass in 14 of 18 variants at or below 1.7 percent of stands above the BA p99.9 by year 50, the SDI ceiling is never exceeded by more than 4.7 percent of stands, and the year-50 lower bound passes in every arm. Under the alternative, CI, CR, WC and WS gain an engine arm and six projector GOMPIT rows become admissible; no projector arm is admissible under the primary rule. Section 15 of the memo has the numbers.

## Stress test of the shipped catalog (October 29, job 15990793)

`stress_catalog.R` recomputes the primary rule from the trajectory-level data and then varies the analyst choices in it, with no new engine runs. Part A is a rule grid of 144 cells: horizon 50 or 100 years, exceedance tolerance 3, 5 or 10 percent, Wilson upper bound 10 or 15 percent, basal area ceiling p99.9 or p99, Reineke flag tolerance 10, 15 or 25 percent, minimum evaluated trajectories 10 or 30. Part B is a stand bootstrap, 500 resamples with replacement per variant under the shipped rule. The recomputed rule reproduces all 72 shipped engine verdicts (zero disagreements), which is an independent check of `catalog_gates.R`. `stress/stress_by_arm.csv` adds `boot_admissible_prob`, `grid_admissible_share`, the verdict under each single-axis change, and `verdict_stability` (STABLE or FRAGILE, admissible or not); `stress/stress_by_variant.csv` gives the probability that a variant keeps at least one admissible engine arm and the share of grid cells in which it does.

Of the 23 admissible arms, 15 are stable and 8 fragile (the ACD GOMPIT arms, CS default GOMPIT, LS calibrated native, the NE calibrated arms, NE default GOMPIT, WS calibrated native); of the 49 not-admissible arms, 41 are stable and 8 fragile (CI default native, CR calibrated native, EC default native, the four SO arms, TT calibrated GOMPIT), which are the arms the alternative Reineke rule admits and the bootstrap keeps below even odds. The seven recommended variants keep an admissible engine arm in 100 percent of resamples (ACD, BM, CS, LS, SN), 99 percent (NE) and 90 percent (WS). The Reineke tolerance is the only axis that changes recommended variants (25 percent admits 13 more arms); the p99 ceiling admits only five arms anywhere; the 100 year horizon removes LS calibrated native; the 3 percent tolerance removes the ACD GOMPIT arms, LS calibrated native and NE default GOMPIT; the minimum of 30 evaluated trajectories changes nothing. No shipped verdict is overturned. Memo Section 16 has the discussion.

## What the catalog does not cover

Per-variant near-term accuracy. The only near-term scorecard is the Northeast cohort in Paper 2, so outside NE the tie-break is simplicity alone and the catalog says so in `nearterm_evidence`. Posterior uncertainty: the calibrated MAP is a point estimate here; the Paper 1 ensemble shows the 100 draw band for NE and ACD. Regeneration: none of the arms establishes regeneration, so a 100 year plateau or decline in stems is partly a design choice. Species groups: the common sample is unstratified, so the Eichhorn, site ordering and Sukachev relations are not part of the catalog gates.
