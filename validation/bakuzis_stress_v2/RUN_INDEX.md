# validation/bakuzis_stress_v2: index (October 29, 2026)

What is in this folder, in the order it was built. Every number in the memo traces to a file named here; md5 lists sit beside each result set.

- `20261027_fvs-modern_fvs-conus_bakuzis_assessment.md`: the running memo, Sections 1 to 16. Section 14 is the regenerated Paper 1 ensemble, Section 15 the CONUS configuration catalog and the GOMPSURV fix, Section 16 the stress test of the shipped catalog. Earlier versions of a section are kept beside it in the outputs folder with a `_PREV_` suffix, never overwritten.
- `bakuzis_stress_v2.R`: the versioned scorer (v2.1 warns when a threshold file matches no data group). Tests T0 shock, T1 monotone, T2 site ordering, T3 Reineke (step-aware, relative-density gated), T3b frontier, T4 magnitude against FIA percentiles, T5 Eichhorn, T6 Sukachev, T7 yield-density, T8 plateau, T9 inversions; reports evaluated, not evaluated and flagged counts for every relation.
- `engine_bakuzis_v2.py`, `engine_v2_firebreather/`: the synthetic 36-cell matrix harness (SITECODE, seeding and site gates, two survival libraries) and the main-branch results on both survival arms.
- `gompsurv_fix/`: the five-argument GOMPSURV defect in 21 variant mortality routines (segfault under GOMPIT on ACD before the fix, 36 of 36 after; native identical before and after). Fixed on main in PR 119.
- `cardinal_scorers/`, `table6_scorer/`, `cardinal_rescore/`: the recovered Paper 2 Table 6 scorer and the October 27 rescore of the four CONUS projector arms (1,500 stands per variant) with the magnitude test.
- `ens_regen_20261028/`: the regenerated Paper 1 FIA-stand ensemble (seed gate, laws table, summary_long, scorecards).
- `catalog/`: the CONUS configuration catalog. `README.md` has the stand sample, thresholds, pre-registered rule, stress-test alternative, red-team repairs and results; `implementation-notes.md` the deviations and open items; `conus_catalog_runs.py` the engine runner (CONDPROP expansion, species relabelling, NOAUTOES flag, checkpoints); `catalog_gates.R` the gates; `catalog_runs_v2.slurm` and `finalize_catalog.slurm` the Cardinal jobs; `patch_config_loader.py` the loader fix applied to `config/config_loader.py`; `diag_overseed.py` the seeding diagnostic; `paper2_fig2_bakuzis.R` the Paper 2 Figure 2 script; `v2/` and `v3/` the result sets (v3 is current, feeding `config/catalog/`); `prep_scorecard_long.R`, `bakuzis_scorecards.R`, `scorecards.slurm` and `scorecards/` the per-variant Bakuzis scorecards (all relations, evaluated and flagged counts, component attribution); `stress_catalog.R`, `stress.slurm` and `stress/` the stress test of the shipped catalog (rule grid and stand bootstrap, job 15990793).
- `paper1_figures_20261029/`: the Paper 1 Bakuzis figures regenerated from the corrected ensemble with `calibration/python/bakuzis_uncertainty_figures.py` (two pandas-version fixes in that script, `.to_numpy()` on plotted series).
- `config/catalog/` (repository root): the catalog the selector `fvs2py.catalog.select_config(variant)` reads.

Pull requests, in order: 112 to 118 (scorer, harness, engine A/B, Table 6 scorer, rescore, generator fix, ensemble regeneration), 119 (GOMPSURV fix), 120 (catalog v1, superseded), 121 (catalog v2 after the output red team), 122 (selector fallback), 123 (catalog v3: loader fix, NOAUTOES, diagnostic, Figure 2), and the scorecards PR that follows.
