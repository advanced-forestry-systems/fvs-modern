### 3.4 Long-run biological realism

The long-run gate scores the four CONUS projector arms, species-free (b1) and species-dependent (b2) diameter growth crossed with GOMPIT and native variant mortality, on the Bakuzis relations the emitted metrics support, with each relation reported as the number of stands on which it was evaluated and the number flagged, and with projected basal area and stand density index tested against the observed 99th and 99.9th percentiles of the FIA fitting data by variant (Table 6). Each arm was scored on a random sample of 1,500 stands per variant (24,097 to 27,097 trajectories per arm) projected 100 years in 5 year cycles. Because the projector emits no dominant height, Eichhorn's rule and site ordering of height could not be evaluated on these runs and are reported as such rather than as passes; the self-thinning test is applied only to stands that reach a relative density of 0.55, which was 9,297 to 14,424 trajectories per arm.

The magnitude test separates the two mortality formulations. Under native variant mortality, 35 percent of b1 stands and 28 percent of b2 stands finish above the observed 99.9th percentile of basal area for their variant, and 22 and 17 percent exceed the observed self-thinning ceiling; under GOMPIT survival the corresponding fractions are 12 and 13 percent, and 5 and 4 percent. The excess is concentrated in the eastern variants. In the b1_native arm 1,444 of 1,500 Northeast stands and 1,472 of 1,500 Southern stands exceed the basal area 99.9th percentile (62.7 and 61.8 m² ha⁻¹), the median peak stand density index reaches 1.23 (NE), 1.50 (SN), 1.33 (CS) and 1.18 (LS) times the observed ceiling, and the within-stand Reineke slopes in those four variants are -0.14, +0.12, -0.37 and -0.14, so stems are not removed as stands densify; the western variants under the same arm carry slopes of -1.6 to -1.7 with few exceedances. Under b2_gompit the Northeast median peak SDI is 0.78 of the ceiling, 188 of 1,500 stands exceed it, and the within-stand slopes run -1.3 to -1.9 across variants (NE -1.57). The pooled upper-edge frontier passes in nearly every variant under every arm (NE -1.91 under b1_native, -1.97 under b2_gompit), which reflects the projector's SDImax ceiling acting on the densest stands and is why the frontier slope alone, the statistic the earlier version of this table reported, was not a sufficient test. The GOMPIT arms carry more single-cycle basal area declines above 10 percent (50 percent of trajectories against 27 to 31 percent under native), which we read as survival arriving in pulses on a 5 year cycle and flag for examination at the cycle level.

The synthetic matrix reported separately for the Northeast engine (36 scenarios of four species groups by three site classes by three initial densities, 100 years, main-branch build) shows neither mortality formulation collapsing or running away: no first-cycle shocks, site ordering and the volume-height relation holding in every group, peak SDI below the engine maximum, and maximum basal area of 55 (GOMPIT) and 62 (native) m² ha⁻¹ against an observed Northeast 99th percentile of 51 m² ha⁻¹ on this fitting data, with GOMPIT the cleaner of the two on the self-thinning axis (3 of 20 flagged against 7 of 23). The realism gate therefore supports GOMPIT survival over native variant mortality in the same direction as the near-term gate, and it says that on real stands in the eastern variants neither formulation yet holds basal area inside the observed envelope, which is the refitting target the two gates together point to.
