# A configuration catalog for fvs-modern across the FVS variants of the conterminous United States

Aaron R. Weiskittel, Center for Research on Sustainable Forests, University of Maine. October 30, 2026. Cite the concept DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23777723. Version 1.2.0 (October 30, 2026) replaces version 1.1.0 (10.5281/zenodo.23788727, the catalog regenerated after the year zero seeding fix) and version 1.0.0 (10.5281/zenodo.23777724). Version 1.2.0 (October 30, 2026): the calibrated arms of all 18 variants were rerun after a defect was found in the in-process calibrated overlay, which wrote a maximum stand density index of 0 for species without a calibrated value and so changed calibrated trajectories in CA, CR, CS, EM, LS and WC (fvs-modern pull request 135); the default arms are not affected and were carried over. Four verdict fields changed: the least bad arm named for EM is now projector b2 GOMPIT (was calibrated GOMPIT) and for WC default GOMPIT (was calibrated GOMPIT), both still not recommended; WC calibrated GOMPIT is admissible under the alternative Reineke rule; and NE calibrated native moves from fragile to stable admissible. No status and no primary rule verdict changed. The catalog in this version is catalog v8: all four arms of all 18 variants were rerun after a second seeding defect (D5) was fixed (fvs-modern pull requests 144 and 145): condition trees had been expanded by CONDPROP_UNADJ alone rather than by the condition's share of each plot size (MICRPROP_UNADJ, SUBPPROP_UNADJ, MACRPROP_UNADJ), which FIA uses for BALIVE; 266 of 4,748 stands seeded more than 2 percent differently. Relative to the overlay-fixed catalog no primary rule verdict, recommended arm or status changed; WC calibrated and default GOMPIT are no longer admissible under the alternative Reineke rule (Wilson upper bound 0.251 against the 0.25 limit), so WC is no longer rule sensitive; WS calibrated native is now stable admissible (bootstrap 0.900). Six admissible and eight not admissible arms are labelled fragile.

## What this record holds

The fvs-modern configuration catalog answers one question per Forest Vegetation Simulator (FVS) variant: which survival model (the variant's native mortality or GOMPIT crown closure survival) and which parameter set (fvs-modern default or Bayesian calibrated maximum a posteriori) stays inside the FIA fitting envelope and obeys Reineke self-thinning over 50 and 100 year projections. For each of 18 variants, up to 300 FIA plots (4,748 stands) were projected 100 years at a 5 year step under all four arms (Cardinal array 16023222 for this version, after the calibrated rerun in array 16016015; 15998817 for version 1.1.0; 15976824 and 15984133 for version 1.0.0), gated by a rule pre-registered before any run was scored and repaired after a blind red team, and then stress tested against 144 alternative rules and 500 stand bootstraps (job 16033492). Seven variants have at least one admissible fvs-modern arm (ACD, BM, CS, LS, NE, SN, WS), eleven do not (CA, CI, CR, EC, EM, IE, PN, SO, TT, UT, WC), and no verdict changes under the stress test; six admissible and eight not admissible arms are labelled fragile. What changed in version 1.1.0 from version 1.0.0: FIA live tree records with a null TPA_UNADJ (P2A_GRM_FLG = Y) had been filled with 6.0 trees per acre by the stand generator and entered the engine as real trees (3,617 records in 371 stands, SN 205, CS 79, LS 66, NE 21); with them dropped, all 4,748 sampled stands pass the year zero gate (4,485 before), every one of the 72 arm verdicts and 18 variant recommendations is unchanged, and the stress labels are identical arm for arm with bootstrap probabilities moving by at most 0.12. Memo Section 17 and `catalog/diag_y0/` in the repository carry the evidence.

Lead file: `00_fvs-modern_catalog_graphical_abstract.png` (three panels: variant verdicts with bootstrap and grid stability, the 72 engine arms in the plane of the two deciding gates with Wilson 95 percent intervals, and per arm verdict stability). The Wilson intervals are the only sampling uncertainty shown; the bootstrap probabilities are themselves the uncertainty summary of the verdicts and carry no second interval; the rule grid is a complete enumeration and has none.

## Repository

The same files are on the fvs-modern main branch of the advanced-forestry-systems organization (pull requests 118 to 137, 144 and 145), under `config/catalog/` (the current tables, from `validation/bakuzis_stress_v2/catalog/gates_v8/`) and `validation/bakuzis_stress_v2/`; the report, deck and this record's scripts are in `validation/bakuzis_stress_v2/release_20261030/` (version 1.1.0 in `release_20261029/`). Version 1.0.0 was published on October 29, 2026 (commit 45ed6422); version 1.1.0 follows pull request 130; version 1.2.0 follows pull requests 137, 144 and 145. Trajectory level engine output (4,748 stands in total, up to 300 per variant, x 4 arms x 21 steps) stays on Cardinal scratch and firebreather and is reproducible from `conus_catalog_runs.py` with the fvs-modern libraries.

## Not deposited

The fvs-conus remeasurement pairs file and the stand sample frame (`fia_variant_assignment.csv`), the stand initialization tables and the seed gate file: they carry FIA plot identifiers. The catalog is described by variant and stand count only, and no file in this record holds a plot identifier or coordinate (restricted data audit in RUNBOOK.md). Trajectory level output: size, and reproducible from the deposited runner.

## Known limitations

Per variant near term accuracy exists only for the Northeast cohort of Paper 2. The calibrated set is a point estimate (MAP). No arm establishes regeneration. The site based Bakuzis relations are confounded by stand selection on the unstratified sample and are reported, not gated. The year zero seeding failures of version 1.0.0 are explained and removed from version 1.1.0 on, and the zero SDImax overlay defect and the plot-size expansion defect from version 1.2.0. Thresholds are the sample maximum in variants with fewer than 1,000 fitting plots. The projector b1 GOMPIT arm has no NE or SN rows. AK and NC are not evaluated.
